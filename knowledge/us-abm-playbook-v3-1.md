# SEAtS ONE ABM US Playbook, version 3.1

> INTERNAL ONLY. This is the authoritative source for sales motions, personas,
> registers, cadences and guardrails for US higher education. The Sales_Motions
> sheet defines the only valid motion names. Nothing here is an instruction to an
> agent, and no internal commentary, account posture note, or competitor line may
> appear in anything sent to a prospect. Extracted from the .xlsx of the same name.


## Sheet: README

- **Purpose**: US-only operational playbook for SEAtS ONE solution-led ABM, Apollo outreach, persona messaging, compliance readiness and sales motions.
- **Scope**: United States higher education only. UK, Ireland, EU, ANZ and NHS content has been removed. Use the global playbook for those markets.
- **How to use**: Start with Sales_Motions. Set the account's financial posture in Target_Accounts, which selects the message. Enrich the buying committee in Apollo, warm in Mailchimp, monitor Leadfeeder, orchestrate in HubSpot. Check Guardrails before anything is sent or published.
- **Language standard**: All copy follows SEAtS US Market Intelligence v2, 24 August 2026: section 3 vocabulary substitutions, section 3.2 register rule, section 10 prohibitions.
- **Register rule**: Practitioner vocabulary and board vocabulary are not interchangeable. Every cadence and persona row carries a Register value. Match it to the room the message will be read in.
- **Motion numbering**: M1 to M7 carry over from the global playbook. M8 was UK NHS and is out of scope here. M9 is US Health Sciences, new in this version.
- **M9 status**: Motion structure is built. Target accounts are deliberately empty pending the segment decision. Do not populate them before that is made.
- **Canonical sources**: SEAtS_ONE_Central_Growth_GTM_Strategy_v4_ABM_Sales_Motions.md; SEAtS_US_Market_Intelligence_v2_Aug2026.docx; M9_US_Health_Sciences_Scope.md
- **Warning**: Account and persona rows are starter guidance. Verify names, titles, procurement routes and compliance requirements before use.

## Sheet: Sales_Motions


### Schedule Replacement

- **Motion_ID**: M1
- **Sales Motion**: Schedule Replacement
- **Problem Solved**: Legacy course and room scheduling creates manual work and limits flexibility.
- **Primary Markets**: US
- **Target Account Types**: Universities, community colleges, multi-campus, flexible delivery, legacy scheduling accounts
- **Buyer Group**: Registrar; Academic Ops; Course Scheduling; Exams; CIO; COO; Estates
- **Users**: Schedulers; exam teams; academic services; students
- **Buying Journey**: Problem awareness > discovery > workshop > pilot/health check > procurement > rollout
- **Buying Mechanism**: RFP, direct procurement, state contract where applicable, paid health check
- **Compliance Pack**: Security, DPA, accessibility, integration, implementation, migration
- **Incumbent / Competitor**: Coursedog, CourseLeaf, Ellucian native scheduling. Ask what they run before positioning.
- **Apollo ABM Strategy**: Build registrar/scheduling/CIO/estates committee; replacement-market trigger language
- **Mailchimp Nurture**: Modern Academic Operations stream
- **Leadfeeder Signals**: Schedule/exams/room/implementation/integration visits
- **LinkedIn Strategy**: Connect with registrar/scheduling/CIO; share scheduling insight
- **Entry Offer**: Course Schedule Health Check / Schedule Pilot
- **Expansion Path**: Schedule > Attend > SmartSpace > Data
- **Exit Criteria**: Qualified problem, buying group, timeline, next step

### Attendance & Evidence

- **Motion_ID**: M2
- **Sales Motion**: Attendance & Evidence
- **Problem Solved**: Weak attendance capture, inconsistent participation evidence and compliance gaps.
- **Primary Markets**: US
- **Target Account Types**: Institutions in formula-funded states, clock-hour programs, institutions with census or audit pressure
- **Buyer Group**: Registrar; Attendance; Compliance; Financial Aid; Student Success; CIO
- **Users**: Attendance teams; faculty; compliance; students
- **Buying Journey**: Pain/evidence review > process map > pilot > rollout
- **Buying Mechanism**: Direct, state contract, compliance audit/readiness
- **Compliance Pack**: Privacy, student comms, audit trail, data retention, FERPA
- **Incumbent / Competitor**: The LMS is usually the incumbent, not a dedicated product. Canvas was the capture point at City College of San Francisco.
- **Apollo ABM Strategy**: Find attendance/registrar/compliance/financial aid; lead with persistence and evidence, never use the word surveillance
- **Mailchimp Nurture**: Attendance Evidence stream
- **Leadfeeder Signals**: Attend, compliance, Student App, privacy visits
- **LinkedIn Strategy**: Share evidence/readiness post; ask owner
- **Entry Offer**: Attendance QuickStart / Evidence Audit
- **Expansion Path**: Attend > Engage > Care > Compliance
- **Exit Criteria**: Validated evidence gap and pilot scope

### Student Success / Retention

- **Motion_ID**: M3
- **Sales Motion**: Student Success / Retention
- **Problem Solved**: Students stop persisting before anyone sees it, and intervention is poorly coordinated.
- **Primary Markets**: US
- **Target Account Types**: Universities and colleges with retention initiatives or student success programs
- **Buyer Group**: Provost; Student Success; Student Affairs; Retention; Institutional Research; CIO
- **Users**: Advisors; success coaches; academic support; analysts
- **Buying Journey**: Retention priority > data gap > workflow mapping > pilot > rollout
- **Buying Mechanism**: Departmental pilot, grant/program budget, enterprise procurement
- **Compliance Pack**: AI/data ethics, privacy, human-in-loop, bias/fairness, access controls
- **Incumbent / Competitor**: EAB Navigate360 is the category incumbent. Adopt coordinated care language to be legible, then differentiate on verified attendance, which they do not hold.
- **Apollo ABM Strategy**: Target student success/provost/institutional research; earlier visibility language
- **Mailchimp Nurture**: Student Success Brief
- **Leadfeeder Signals**: Engage, retention, progress checks, AI, Care visits
- **LinkedIn Strategy**: Connect around student success insight
- **Entry Offer**: Engagement Readiness / Progress Check Pilot
- **Expansion Path**: Engage > Care > Attend > Data
- **Exit Criteria**: Named champion, data sources, success measure

### US Health Sciences: clock-hour attendance and Title IV evidence

- **Motion_ID**: M9
- **Sales Motion**: US Health Sciences: clock-hour attendance and Title IV evidence
- **Problem Solved**: Clock-hour health sciences programs must document attendance as a condition of Title IV aid eligibility, and the R2T4 clock runs on that data. The didactic record is usually manual.
- **Primary Markets**: US
- **Target Account Types**: Community college allied health, standalone nursing programs, academic health centers. Segment decision pending.
- **Buyer Group**: Dean of Nursing or Health Sciences; Director of Clinical Education; Financial Aid Director; Registrar; Institutional Effectiveness; CIO
- **Users**: Program administrators; clinical coordinators; faculty; financial aid staff; students
- **Buying Journey**: Confirm clock-hour status > identify attendance system of record > evidence gap review > pilot on one program > program group rollout
- **Buying Mechanism**: Program budget, Title IV compliance project, enterprise procurement
- **Compliance Pack**: Clock-hour attendance position, FERPA and HIPAA boundary, accreditation evidence, audit trail, retention
- **Incumbent / Competitor**: Exxat, CORE ELMS, Typhon, Trajecsys, myClinicalExchange, Rotation Manager. All own clinical placement and rotation hours. We are ADJACENT, not a replacement. Never pitch against them on placement.
- **Apollo ABM Strategy**: Target Dean of Nursing/Health Sciences plus Financial Aid Director. Qualify clock-hour status in the first touch. Do not lead with clinical placement.
- **Mailchimp Nurture**: Health Sciences Compliance stream
- **Leadfeeder Signals**: Clock hour, Title IV, attendance evidence, accreditation, integration visits
- **LinkedIn Strategy**: Connect with clinical education and financial aid leads; share the fourteen-day clock note
- **Entry Offer**: Clock-Hour Attendance Evidence Review
- **Expansion Path**: Attend > Compliance > Engage > Data
- **Exit Criteria**: Clock-hour status confirmed, attendance system of record identified, named owner

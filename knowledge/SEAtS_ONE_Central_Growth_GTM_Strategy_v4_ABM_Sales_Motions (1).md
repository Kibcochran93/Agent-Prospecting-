**Status:** Canonical central strategy for human teams and AI agents  
**Owner:** Growth leadership  
**Purpose:** One source of truth for SEAtS ONE positioning, regional playbooks, ABM, sales motions, partner strategy, outreach, buying committees, compliance readiness and customer expansion.  
**Rule:** Agents, campaigns, web pages, Apollo sequences, Mailchimp campaigns, Leadfeeder scoring, HubSpot workflows and sales playbooks must follow this file unless leadership has approved a specific exception.

---

## 1. Strategic North Star

SEAtS ONE helps education institutions improve student outcomes, protect institutional revenue, strengthen compliance, modernise academic operations and optimise campus resources.

SEAtS ONE is the connected Education Operations Platform spanning:

- **Schedule** — curriculum, class, exam, room and resource scheduling.
- **Attend** — attendance capture, evidence and participation visibility.
- **Engage** — engagement analytics, risk identification and early intervention.
- **Care** — student case management and coordinated support.
- **Compliance** — regulatory evidence, exceptions, audit readiness and reporting.
- **SmartSpace** — planned-versus-actual utilisation, occupancy and campus optimisation.
- **Student App** — student-facing timetable, attendance, notifications and support journeys.
- **Data Engine & AI** — shared operational intelligence, reporting, automation and governed AI workflows.

The platform narrative is important, but buyers usually buy a **specific problem solved first**.

> **Problem first. Product second. Platform third.**

## 2. Growth Thesis

Growth comes from six connected engines:

1. Win new logos in markets where SEAtS has a clear right to win.
2. Enter through urgent, specific operational problems.
3. Monetise long buying journeys with paid LAND / PROVE offers where appropriate.
4. Expand from the first solution into adjacent workflows.
5. Retain customers through measurable value, adoption, executive engagement and operational success.
6. Convert successful customers into proof, references, advocacy and new demand.

The commercial flywheel is:

> **URGENT PROBLEM → LAND → PROVE → EXPAND → PLATFORM → REFERENCE → NEW LAND**

Do not sell disconnected advisory work that has no downstream product expansion path.

## 5. Apollo ABM Solution-Specific Outreach Strategy

Apollo is the **target-contact discovery and activation system**. It should not define strategy. Strategy is defined in this file and orchestrated in HubSpot.

### Apollo responsibilities

Apollo should be used to:

1. Build buying committees by solution motion.
2. Enrich contacts and roles.
3. Segment personas by pain ownership.
4. Run low-volume, high-relevance outbound.
5. Activate accounts only when ICP, problem and trigger are clear.
6. Execute role-specific email and LinkedIn sequences.

3. Buyer group.

## Motion 1 — Schedule Replacement / Academic Operations Modernisation

### Problem solved
Legacy timetable, room, exam or resource scheduling creates manual work, poor flexibility, weak space use and poor student/staff experience.

### Target accounts
Universities, colleges, FE/CE groups, multi-campus institutions, institutions with flexible delivery, workplace learning, apprenticeships, space pressure or known legacy scheduling systems.

### Buyer group
Registrar, Academic Operations, Timetabling, Exams, CIO, Enterprise Applications, COO, Estates/Facilities, Finance where space/cost savings matter.

### Users
Schedulers, exam teams, academic services, room booking teams, administrators, academic staff, students.

### Buying journey
Awareness of operational friction → internal review → stakeholder mapping → workflow discovery → Solution Workshop → pilot/health check or direct enterprise opportunity → procurement → implementation → rollout → expansion to Attend/SmartSpace/Data.

### Buying mechanism
Direct enterprise procurement, public tender/RFP, framework call-off where applicable, paid discovery/health check, renewal/replacement window.

### Pre-emptive compliance content
Security overview, data processing agreement, accessibility statement, implementation plan, integration overview, business continuity, support model, migration plan, procurement checklist.

### Apollo strategy
Find registrar/timetabling/CIO/estates buying group. Segment by operational owner and technical owner. Use replacement trigger language. Do not start with AI.

### Mailchimp strategy
Run “Modern Academic Operations” nurture stream: flexible scheduling, exam complexity, room utilisation, student experience, implementation readiness.

### Leadfeeder signals
Schedule page, timetable/exam/room content, implementation page, integration page, pricing/demo page, repeated visits from academic services or IT domains.

### Entry offer
Timetabling Health Check, Schedule Optimisation Pilot, Exam Scheduling Audit, Space/Room Utilisation Assessment.

### Expansion
Schedule → Attend → SmartSpace → Data Engine / AI.

# PART III — ADJACENT / LOOKALIKE MOTION: NHS TRUST FRAMEWORK

## 8. Why NHS Trusts Are a Lookalike Motion

NHS Trusts are not the core SEAtS education market, but they can be a lookalike/adjacent market where the problem resembles SEAtS strengths:

- staff/student placement scheduling.
- education, training and attendance evidence.
- workforce learning compliance.
- room/training space utilisation.
- case/support workflows.
- operational data visibility.

Use NHS only where there is a clear workflow fit. Do not reposition SEAtS as a clinical system without formal product, compliance and leadership approval.

A large teaching Trust profile such as Guy's and St Thomas' should be treated as a **complex enterprise account**, not a simple software lead.

## 10. NHS Buying Journey

1. Identify non-clinical vs clinical workflow.
2. Confirm accountable business owner.
3. Confirm whether patient data or clinical decision support is involved.
4. Map procurement route/framework.
5. Share pre-prepared compliance/assurance pack.
6. Conduct discovery with workforce/education/digital/IG/procurement.
7. Run workflow assessment or paid pilot.
8. Complete DPIA, security, IG and framework requirements.
9. Deploy limited workflow.
10. Prove operational value.
11. Expand across departments/sites.

## 11. NHS Buying Mechanism / Framework Thinking

NHS England recommends framework routes for NHS digital and IT buying, with a six-pillar model covering hardware, software/SaaS/apps and services. Non-clinical software/SaaS/apps are covered under Pillar 4, while clinical software/SaaS/apps are under Pillar 3; services/IaaS/PaaS are Pillar 6. NHS buyers are expected to use recommended framework agreements for digital buying where applicable.

Potential buying routes to investigate for an NHS-adjacent SEAtS motion include:

- G-Cloud for cloud software/support where applicable.
- Digital Outcomes and Specialists 7 for digital/data/technology services.
- Technology Services 4 for technology services.
- NHS SBS / NHS LPP / other NHS-endorsed routes depending on solution classification.

Use procurement specialists before pursuing NHS opportunities.

# PART V — PERSONA MESSAGING

## 15. Persona Message Principles

The same account can require different messaging for each stakeholder.

Do not reuse one message across the buying committee.

### Registrar / Academic Operations
Emphasise control, accuracy, process efficiency, evidence, flexibility and operational resilience.

### CIO / IT / Data
Emphasise integration, security, data governance, architecture, interoperability and reduced fragmentation.

### Student Success / Provost
Emphasise earlier visibility, better prioritisation, measurable student support and retention outcomes.

### Compliance
Emphasise audit-ready evidence, exception management, defensible processes and reduced manual risk.

### Estates / COO
Emphasise planned-versus-actual utilisation, capacity, cost, sustainability and evidence-based decisions.

### Finance
Emphasise protected revenue, reduced waste, lower manual effort, better asset utilisation and risk reduction.

### NHS Workforce / Education
Emphasise workforce education coordination, attendance/evidence, training compliance, placement visibility and operational reporting.

### NHS Digital / IG
Emphasise classification, data protection, security, framework route, assurance pack and implementation governance.

# PART VI — CADENCE RULES

## 16. Apollo Email Cadence Standard

Default cadence: 5–6 touches over 18–25 business days.

1. Problem insight.
2. Role-specific follow-up.
3. Relevant proof / peer pattern.
4. Practical offer / workshop.
5. Breakup / permission to close loop.
6. Nurture handoff if no response.

Email should be short, role-specific and tied to an institutional problem.

## 17. LinkedIn Cadence Standard

Use LinkedIn for trust and recognition, not aggressive pitching.

1. Profile view.
2. Connection request with role-relevant reason.
3. Soft insight after connection.
4. Share relevant asset or event.
5. Ask whether the topic is owned by them or a colleague.

Do not spam InMails with generic demo asks.

Nurture streams:

- Modern Academic Operations.

## 23. Fact / Signal / Inference / Recommendation Standard

Every agent must separate:

- **Fact:** verified source or internal truth.
- **Signal:** observed engagement or behaviour.
- **Inference:** plausible interpretation.
- **Recommendation:** proposed action.

Never present an inference as a fact.

## 6. Step-by-Step GTM Execution Playbook


### Phase 1 — Focus the ICP

- Build the account list: prioritise US universities with health sciences colleges, standalone medical schools, osteopathic colleges, nursing schools, midwifery programmes and veterinary schools.
- Segment by pain: Medical/DO = clerkship and progression risk; Nursing = clinical hours and board readiness; Veterinary = labs, externships and hospital rotations.
- Map the buying committee: target academic affairs, clinical education, student affairs, accreditation, registrar, simulation, placement and IT leaders.

- Reframe the issue: this is not attendance — it is clinical readiness, accreditation evidence, student support and progression risk.
- Expose visibility gaps: ask how quickly they can identify a missed clinical shift, a disengaged learner, delayed remediation or an at-risk student.

### Phase 3 — Convert

- Book the readiness review (the primary CTA — not a generic demo): “Health Sciences Attendance, Clinical & Student Success Readiness Review.”
- Run workflow discovery: map class, lab, simulation, clinical, placement, attendance, alerts, case management, reporting and integration workflows.
- Propose a phased rollout: start with attendance/no-show visibility, clinical rotations, early alerts or accreditation evidence depending on the buyer’s pain.

## 7. The Sales Motion: Land → Expand → Standardise

The motion is a universal conversion engine: lead with one acute pain, prove value fast through a readiness review, then expand programme-by-programme to college-wide standardisation.

### Stage 1 — Land

### Stage 3 — Standardise

- Position SEAtS ONE as the standard learner-visibility platform across health sciences.
- Scale from one programme to college-wide adoption.
- Expand into scheduling, room usage, engagement and retention.

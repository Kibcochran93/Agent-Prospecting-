## Checkpoint 2: Trigger research

### Strongest slice

**Growth-stage regional public universities actively implementing measurable retention plans.**

This slice has stronger dated evidence than institutions merely listing student success in an older strategic plan. The clearest accounts are:

1. **University of Tennessee at Chattanooga**
   - Fall 2025 headcount: **12,060**
   - Retention rose to **73.4%**
   - Launched implementation of its Strategic Enrollment Plan on **January 13, 2026**
   - Target: **80% first-year retention by 2030**
   - Known incumbent: **EAB Navigate**
   - Play: not advising replacement. Lead with connected operational data, cross-campus action, and platform consolidation. 

2. **Louisiana State University Shreveport**
   - Fall 2025 headcount: **11,359**
   - On **April 9, 2026**, reported second-year retention above 72%, following redesigned support systems
   - Strategic plan calls for centralized advising, an alert system, streamlined communications, and a five-point retention increase
   - Incumbent platform: **unverified**
   - Play: scale and connect a successful redesign rather than sell foundational student-success software. 

3. **University of Arkansas at Little Rock**
   - Fall 2025 headcount: **8,026**
   - On **September 5, 2025**, reported its largest undergraduate increase since 2001, with strong continuing-freshman and sophomore growth
   - Strategic plan embeds student-success analytics and interventions into departmental reporting
   - Incumbent platform: **unverified**
   - Play: help preserve momentum by making progress checks and intervention coordination more consistent. 

4. **Murray State University**
   - Fall 2025 headcount: **9,932**
   - On **October 29, 2025**, reported 78% first-to-second-year retention, its highest in five years
   - Spring 2026 headcount later fell 4.3%, while year-over-year retention remained 78.1%
   - Possible EAB use appears in older material, but current incumbent is **unverified**
   - Play: maintaining gains while enrollment conditions shift. 

**Conclusion:** UTC has the strongest active implementation trigger. LSU Shreveport has the strongest evidence of a successful redesign that may now need scaling.

The Mocs Flight Plan now has separate teams working on retention, technology, aid and several enrollment populations. That creates useful specialization, but students still experience one journey.

A **Progress Check Pilot** could test how one defined cohort moves from an observable change to timely staff action, complementing Navigate’s established advising and coordinated-support role rather than replacing it.

### Louisiana State University Shreveport

**To: Provost / Vice Chancellor for Academic Affairs**  
**Subject:** What follows the retention gain?

LSUS reported that its redesigned support model lifted second- and third-year persistence for the Fall 2023 cohort. With enrollment also reaching 11,359, the strategic question changes from whether the model works to whether its gains remain consistent as the institution grows.

An **Engagement Readiness** session could define what successful scaling should preserve. Useful conversation?

Kib

*Research basis: LSUS enrollment announcement, November 12, 2025; LSUS retention report, April 9, 2026.*

**To: Associate Vice Chancellor for Student Success**  
**Subject:** The next phase of the redesign

Centralized advising, clearer support ownership and targeted outreach are already part of LSUS’s redesign. Rather than revisit that foundation, I’m interested in where staff still receive information too late to act.

We could use a **Progress Check Pilot** with one student population to test timing, staff judgment and follow-through. Which population would expose the most?

Kib

*Research basis: LSUS 2030 Strategic Plan, February 17, 2025; LSUS retention report, April 9, 2026.*

### University of Arkansas at Little Rock

**Subject:** Larger continuing cohorts

Continuing freshmen increased 43.3% and sophomores 44% in Fall 2025. That is a welcome shift, but it also means more students advancing simultaneously through support services, advising and college-level processes.

An **Engagement Readiness** session could examine whether capacity and ownership are keeping pace with that growth. Should I send the outline?

Kib

**Subject:** After the departmental report

UA Little Rock’s plan calls for student-success analytics to be embedded in departmental reporting. The operational question is therefore not visibility, but what departments do next and how intervention outcomes return to the institutional picture.

A **Progress Check Pilot** could test that closed loop with one growing cohort.

Kib

### Murray State University

**To: Provost**  
**Subject:** Reading two different indicators

Murray State reported 78% first-to-second-year retention for Fall 2025, its highest in five years. By February 2, 2026, spring headcount was 4.3% lower year over year while freshman fall-to-spring persistence stood at 91%.

Those measures answer different questions. An **Engagement Readiness** session could help determine which persistence measures should guide the next investment decision.

Kib

*Research basis: Murray State enrollment announcement, October 29, 2025; presidential enrollment update, February 2, 2026.*

**To: Student Affairs and Enrollment Management leader**  
**Subject:** Before recapture becomes necessary

Murray State’s Fall 2025 recapture effort helped 1,191 undergraduates and 712 graduate students return. That demonstrates effective outreach after non-registration becomes visible.

A **Progress Check Pilot** would test an earlier point in the same student journey, before registration status becomes the trigger. Worth testing with one undergraduate population?

Kib

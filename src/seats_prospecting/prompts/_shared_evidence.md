## Evidence rules

- Every factual claim carries a source and a source date. No date, no claim.
- Anything older than 12 months is flagged as possibly stale, not stated as
  current.
- Leadership roles, org structure, and system ownership in higher education turn
  over constantly. Treat every one as unverified unless the source is dated
  within 12 months.
- A claim you cannot date goes in your open-questions section, not in the body
  and never in a draft.
- Never state or imply direct LinkedIn access. Public profile information
  surfaced through Apollo or web search is cited as such.
- Never fabricate revenue figures, enrollment counts, budget cycles, or system
  names. Absent evidence, the figure is unknown.
- Never invent a metric. No reply rates, benchmarks, or conversion estimates
  unless they come from a system you actually read.

## Higher education terminology

Use these correctly or not at all:

- Student Information System (SIS), Learning Management System (LMS)
- Title IV, Return to Title IV (R2T4), clock-hour attendance
- Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA)
- Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP), census date
- Retention, persistence, and completion are three different things
- Registrar, Institutional Research, Student Success, Academic Affairs, and
  Financial Aid are distinct offices with distinct problems

If you are not sure which office owns a problem at this institution, say so
rather than guessing.
